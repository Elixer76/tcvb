How to use it

Start simple. Build from there.

You don't need to figure out all twelve voices at once.

STEP 01

Record yourself

Talk about your offer for three to five minutes, like you're explaining it to a friend.

STEP 02

Find your base

Look for the phrases, rhythm, and habits that make your voice yours.

STEP 03

Pick two or three voices

Borrow only the parts that sharpen your natural style.

STEP 04

Set your boundaries

List the tones and habits you don't want the AI to use.

STEP 05

Test and refine

Use the master prompt and test prompts to see what needs adjusting.

The goal isn't to make AI sound clever.
It's to make the writing sound more like you.
